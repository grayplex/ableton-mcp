    - pad: Eq8 (gentle low cut ~100Hz, air boost ~10kHz) + Compressor2 (gentle, slow attack) + Reverb (large room, 2.5s decay) + StereoGain (wide stereo)

    - vocal: Eq8 (presence boost ~2-5kHz, low cut ~80Hz) + Compressor2 (moderate 3:1) + Reverb (plate-like, 1.2s) + ProxyAudioEffectDevice (subtle) + StereoGain (center-ish) + Gate (clean up noise)
    - atmospheric: Eq8 (gentle, wide) + Reverb (long tail 3s+) + ProxyAudioEffectDevice (long feedback) + StereoGain (very wide)
    - return: Reverb (send effect, long, 100% wet) + ProxyAudioEffectDevice (send effect, 100% wet) + StereoGain (unity gain)
    - master: Eq8 (gentle broad curve) + StereoGain (unity gain) — minimal per research recommendation, Phase 34 adds full master chain
